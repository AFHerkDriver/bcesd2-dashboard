Lights ON = attached to a live call + more than ~150 m from the scene. Arrival (<150 m and stopped ~30 s) ends the wig-wag and starts the breathing halo that alternates BLUE, then RED — the lightbar colors cycling around the badge = unit working that scene (command rigs breathe GREEN instead, and green outranks blue/red). The halo ends — and everything goes steady — when the call ends or DISREGARD, the unit detaches, or the unit leaves the scene radius; wig-wag also ends on no movement for 5 min while attached, or the 30-minute backstop. Turnaround (future metrics): attached + closing on scene, then distance climbs ~400 m past its minimum and keeps climbing 45–60 s without ever arriving = cleared en route — lights off, normal icon, and log the handoff timestamp for metrics (distance FLAT is staging, not a turnaround; skip inference on geo-suspect calls). Returning after clearing = normal icon, no lights, no special state. Command rigs (BC/AC/DC): responding wig-wags red/blue only, greens dark; on arrival the mains go dark and the greens pulse — green on the map = command established at that scene, ending when the call closes or the rig leaves. Stacking: command rigs always render on top when units overlap at a scene — and command-established on top of other command rigs. Stale GPS dims and overrides everything. prefers-reduced-motion disables the animation. Placement: dashboard = always on; MDT = optional layer in the layers control. Fleet lightbar families (per the icon sheet): GREEN-end bar = AC/DC/BC (command logic above) · plain BLUE/WHITE/RED bar = UAV, ADM, UTL (2-light wig-wag; in practice you'll mostly see UAV) · AMBER bar = MX/Maintenance (never runs code — steady badge only, no wig-wag state) · MULTICOLOR red-blue-white-blue-red bar = all typical apparatus incl. MOF (ENG, MOF, MED, TDR, LDR, SQD, RES, BRS, WMD, AP — 4-light criss-cross).
