Lights ON = attached to a live call + more than ~150 m from the scene. Lights OFF = arrival (<150 m and stopped ~30 s), call ends or DISREGARD, unit detached, no movement for 5 min while attached, or the 30-minute backstop. Command rigs (BC/AC/DC): responding wig-wags red/blue only, greens dark; on arrival the mains go dark and the greens pulse — green on the map = command established at that scene, ending when the call closes or the rig leaves. Stacking: command rigs always render on top when units overlap at a scene — and command-established on top of other command rigs. Stale GPS dims and overrides everything. prefers-reduced-motion disables the animation.
