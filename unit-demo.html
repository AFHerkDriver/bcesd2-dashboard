Lights ON = attached to a live call + more than ~150 m from the scene. Arrival (<150 m and stopped ~30 s) ends the wig-wag and starts the lightbar slow-breath — blue and red swelling alternately on the bar itself = unit working that scene (command rigs breathe GREEN instead, and green outranks blue/red). The halo ends — and everything goes steady — when the call ends or DISREGARD, the unit detaches, or the unit leaves the scene radius; wig-wag also ends on no movement for 5 min while attached, or the 30-minute backstop. Turnaround (future metrics): attached + closing on scene, then distance climbs ~400 m past its minimum and keeps climbing 45–60 s without ever arriving = cleared en route — lights off, normal icon, and log the handoff timestamp for metrics (distance FLAT is staging, not a turnaround; skip inference on geo-suspect calls). Returning after clearing = normal icon, no lights, no special state. Command rigs (BC/AC/DC): run their family's normal icon and responding wig-wag — BC on the apparatus badge (criss-cross), AC/DC on the plain bar like UAV (2-light); on arrival the on-scene halo is GREEN instead of blue/red — green on the map = command established at that scene, ending when the call closes or the rig leaves. Stacking: command rigs always render on top when units overlap at a scene — and command-established on top of other command rigs. Stale GPS dims and overrides everything. prefers-reduced-motion disables the animation. Placement: dashboard = always on; MDT = optional layer in the layers control. Labels match CAD: the designator you see on dispatch (E121, M121, AP124, BC121, UAV121…) is CSS text drawn on a blank plate — one badge asset per lightbar family, any unit string, auto-shrunk until it clears the plate margins so no designator ever touches the badge edge. Shared rigs carry slash-separated callsigns in the roster (MOF123/UAV121): dispatch matching accepts ANY of them, the badge wears the one the call was assigned to, and idle wears the first listed. Unit roster (fleet.html — built, linked from the control panel): KV-backed via the worker’s /roster endpoints. Units grouped by type, listed by dept reg (###-## fleet number + year, what Samsara reports); per unit: radio callsign (manual, once) + icon family (auto from callsign prefix, overridable) + home station + a 3-way visibility toggle per unit: ALWAYS (mainline apparatus — plots whenever reporting) · ON-CALL (support/staff rigs — plots only while actively attached to a call, vanishes when it clears) · OFF (hidden everywhere). Defaults by family: apparatus = ALWAYS, everything else = ON-CALL; changes are KV state, live on all boards instantly, no deploy. Home-station hiding: an ALWAYS unit disappears only while sitting at its OWN station (~150 m geofence) and not attached to a call — E122 visiting station 121 still plots, and any unit attached to a call plots even from its own bay. Home station may be a slash list (121/124): the rig hides idle at ANY of its listed stations. Station docking: a unit that plots while inside any station’s geofence (~150 m) — a visiting rig, or one attached to a call and still in the bay — never covers the station shield: it docks in a tidy row lined up BELOW the station icon (callsign order, wrapping if several), and snaps back to its raw GPS position the moment it leaves the geofence. Feed vehicles not in the roster surface as “needs callsign” and never plot untagged; rostered units that stop reporting get a no-GPS flag. Icon assignment: plain BLUE/WHITE/RED bar = UAV, ADM, UTL, AC, DC (2-light wig-wag) · AMBER bar = MX/Maintenance (never runs code — steady badge only, no wig-wag state) · MULTICOLOR red-blue-white-blue-red bar = BC + all typical apparatus incl. MOF (BC, ENG, MOF, MED, TDR, LDR, SQD, RES, BRS, WMD, AP — 4-light criss-cross). Command is marked by the GREEN on-scene halo, not a special badge (the green-end blank stays in the repo unused).
